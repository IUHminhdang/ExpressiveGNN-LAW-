# Final Legal KG–ExpressGNN Experiment Report

## 1. Research task and relation meaning

The target task is link prediction for `relatedTo(Article, Article)`. This
relation is the dataset's explicit content-relatedness annotation. It is
directional in the exported graph and is not interpreted as a citation,
legal applicability, amendment, repeal, or symmetric relation.

## 2. Dataset selection

The full Hugging Face corpus contains approximately 65,967 records. The
experiment uses the coherent subset satisfying:

* `topic_title == "Transport"`
* `subject_title == "Inland waterway transport"`

This selection contains 926 records. The full corpus was not grounded because
the estimated grouped rule workload is substantially larger and has not been
validated as safe on this Windows/CPU environment.

## 5. Rules and export contract

The official `load_method=1` bundle contains background facts in `facts.txt`,
target-relation positives in `train.txt`, `valid.txt`, and `test.txt`, and
rules whose final atom is `relatedTo`:

```text
0.5 !belongsToTopic(A, T) v !belongsToTopic(B, T) v relatedTo(A, B)
0.5 !belongsToSubject(A, S) v !belongsToSubject(B, S) v relatedTo(A, B)
```

These are soft heuristic constraints: shared topic or shared subject is
evidence that two articles may be related. They are not legally established
implications and no hard logical constraints were introduced. The official
loader accepted the generated bundle.

## 6. Evaluator audit

For each positive test triple, the official evaluator creates two directional
queries: one replacing the tail and one replacing the head. For
`relatedTo`, both candidate sets are restricted to the 926 Article entities.
The experiment therefore has 11 positive test queries and 22 directional
rankings.

MRR is the mean reciprocal rank over those directional rankings. Hits is
Hits@10. The evaluator's current ranking is observed/unfiltered: other known
positive triples are not removed from the candidate list. `negative_samples.csv`
is generated by the pipeline for audit/baseline use and is not consumed by the
official trainer.

The training log's `observed: 89, hidden: 857387` counts grounded rule heads
already present or absent in observed facts. It is not a train/test count.

## 7. Results

### Popularity baseline

The degree-popularity baseline uses the same 926 Article candidates:

* MRR: **0.2065**
* Hits@10: **0.2273**

### ExpressGNN

Configuration: CPU, 3 configured epochs, batch size 32, 10 batches, seed 42,
and the official `load_method=1` path. The run completed and generated
checkpoints, a training log, and a performance report.

* MRR: **0.0331**
* Hits@10: **0.1364**
* Evaluated directions: **22**

These results are preliminary: the test set contains only 11 positive edges,
and the official evaluator is unfiltered. They should not be presented as
strong evidence that the model is inferior or superior without a larger,
validated test set.

## 8. Grounding and scalability

The following estimates are `sum(group_size^2)` over topic and subject groups
in the selected prefixes of the dataset. They are workload indicators, not
completed full-corpus runs:

| Records | Estimated grouped combinations |
|---:|---:|
| 926 selected subset | 857,476 observed/hidden-scale combinations reported by the run |
| 1,000 | 1,245,326 |
| 5,000 | 11,334,854 |
| 10,000 | 20,764,132 |
| 65,967 full corpus | 230,843,406 |

The full corpus also has an Article-pair upper bound of approximately
4.35 billion ordered pairs. These estimates justify stopping at the
validated domain subset rather than claiming a full-corpus experiment.

## 9. Limitations and threats to validity

1. The test set is very small (11 positives).
2. Ranking is unfiltered, which can penalize a correct alternative that is
   also a known positive.
3. Citation resolution is incomplete (36.51%).
4. Amendment, supplementation, and repeal relations are preserved but
   excluded from official training pending stronger source identity parsing.
5. The rules are heuristic and should not be interpreted as legal reasoning.
6. Only the inland-waterway subject subset was trained; results do not
   generalize to the full corpus.
7. CPU runtime and memory behavior for the full corpus remain unverified.

## 10. Reproduction

From the repository root:

```powershell
python .\kaggle_train.py `
  --bundle .\legal_kg_pipeline\inland_waterway_bundle `
  --expressgnn .\ExpressGNN `
  --experiment .\legal_kg_pipeline\inland_waterway_run_seed42 `
  --epochs 3 --num-batches 10 --batchsize 32 --seed 42 --device cpu
```

The verified run artifacts are under
`legal_kg_pipeline/inland_waterway_run_seed42/`, including
`evaluation_results.json`, `run_config.json`, the training log, checkpoint,
and performance report.
